## 2. How to Read the Evidence Column

From the BenchLM methodology page:

- "Supported and Estimated labels show how much evidence stands behind a position, and an estimated rank remains uncertain."
- "Missing tests remain blank; the estimator never adds benchmark values."
- Estimated positions combine the model's own signals, a calibrated benchmark estimate, a lineage prior and eligible peers.
- "Estimated Overall ranges describe reconstruction error under the tested assumptions... These ranges do not establish confidence in a rank, a future score, or the ordering of the whole board."

In the top 30 above, 9 entries are Estimated-ranked: Gemini 4 Carbon (1), GPT-6.1 Terra (9), Kimi K3.1 Pro (13), GPT-5.5 Pro (14), Muse Wonder Full (18), GPT-5.4 Pro (20), Claude Opus 4.7 (Adaptive) (21), GPT-5.2 Pro (26) and Ling 3.1 Flash (29).
